Activity 3: Resilience & Future Options Simulation

Manage annual budgets across Operating Baseline, National Priority Sectors and financial resilience.

Maximise impact while building capacity to fund future strategic opportunities.

How to play

  1. At the start of each year, complete the budget allocation pop-up below the score cards. Allocate the annual Unallocated Budget and, where required, CLM Agility Fund/Retained Earnings could be used to fund the Operating Baseline
  2. In Year 6, read the budget-reset message first, then proceed to the Year-Start Decision. Select Budget done to finalise the allocation and open the event decision area.
  3. CLM Agility Fund is available only during Years 1 to 5. Any Year 5 closing balance is fully utilised before Year 6. No CLM Agility Fund balance is transferred to Retained Earnings.
  4. Within each Event Card, choose Pass, Invest with CLM Agility Fund, or Invest with Retained Earnings.
  5. Retained Earnings investment requires Board Exco approval.
